# Seed review: clearer prompts and realistic difficulty

Date: 1 November 2026. Revision three refines the 100-entry research seed after the
multi-word rebalance. Sixteen entries changed or were replaced. All remain
evidence-checked, with zero publication approvals. Finished grids and cold solving are
still required.

## What changed

- Replaced Camp Taurajo with **The Crossroads**. The old clue depended on knowing Jorn
  Skyseer. The replacement uses the hub's role for Horde recruits, with
  plainstrider-beak errands as a second route. The quest chain supports both prompts.
  The answer stays recognisable without requiring a minor NPC's name.
- Reworked hints for FATHER, QUEEN, KING, PRINCE, STONE, ICE, SKULL, Night Elf and Blood
  Elf. They now use family, history or transformation context rather than relying
  entirely on direct definitions. Short footholds such as BAT and INN keep their
  generous semantic hints. Context is useful only when it helps the solver; these
  changes still need that practical check.
- Broadened Razorfen Kraul's main clue from a boss reference to a **quilboar
  stronghold**, retaining Charlga Razorflank as an association. Its hint adds the
  Barrens and blueleaf tubers. It remains medium and a particular cold-solve target
  because dungeon/faction experience varies.
- Moved EARTH, SLEEP, SKULL, Ragnaros, Hyjal, Dire Maul and Night Elf from easy to
  medium. Those clues ask for narrower lore, geography or terminology even when the
  answers themselves are familiar. Answer familiarity was preserved.
- Added exact evidence for the new factual hints and replacement prompts, removed unused
  citations, and refreshed only the affected source-review digests after inspecting the
  passages. No cold-review approvals were added.

## Assessment and next check

This is a more varied and better grounded starting bank. It remains a small,
Vanilla-sourced sample within the chosen through-Wrath scope. Expansion needs additional
reviewed sources for TBC/Wrath, and construction may expose missing short vocabulary or
awkward letter patterns.

Do not keep polishing this list indefinitely before testing the product. The next useful
evidence is a reproducible fill experiment, followed by seven completed puzzles played
by people with different faction/class backgrounds. Check whether the clues are
sufficient, the hints help, the crossings are fair, and the solve feels enjoyable. The
[generated inventory](seed-report.md) is the current triad list;
[revision two](seed-revision.md) preserves the earlier selection rationale.
