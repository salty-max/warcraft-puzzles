## Interpretation

The mix is two Easy, three Medium and two Hard candidates. New labels are provisional.
Hard combines deeper lore associations with familiar answers and easier footholds,
rather than making every clue obscure. Grid sizes and checking also vary, so solve-time
differences cannot be assigned to clue wording alone.

The two existing solve identities remain unchanged. Each artifact uses its own puzzle ID
and content fingerprint. The compact index is navigation only, with no new reward or
account system.

## Construction and sources

All answers come from the existing 342-answer bank. New clue/hint variants use reviewed
existing passages, not unreferenced recollections or later-era facts. Source coverage
remains primarily Vanilla and does not constitute a complete TBC/Wrath audit.

Word-led exploratory searches were followed by source reading, clue writing,
cross-prompt review and independent layout/fill validation. Rejected search attempts are
not scored as successful puzzles. Exact candidate layouts and placements are committed;
the exploratory scratch scripts are not a production generator.

Editorial time was not measured with a production stopwatch. A manual five-puzzle batch
is not evidence of a sustainable daily pipeline. The next assessment needs actual
cold-solve results, measured construction/editing effort and repeat spacing over a
longer sample.
