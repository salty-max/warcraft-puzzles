## Review and feedback

Fifteen entries use existing familiar answers, with no answer overlap with the first
puzzle. Fifteen alternate triads retain traceable facts and hints. Eleven clues are
provisionally medium, four easy footholds. The overall Medium label is a hypothesis
until a blind solve.

Difficulty comes from less direct references, relationships and double meanings. No
obscure loot, quest-title recall or fabricated facts were added. Some clues can still be
solved through ordinary English or known game mechanics.

The same construction floor and player apply to both trials. Actual checking differs
between layouts and is reported below. Its longest entry has unchecked stretches, which
need human assessment.

## Benchmark interpretation

Partially checked grids are the primary format. The fill benchmark now includes this
exact layout and independently searches the bank for a complete fill. The authored
placement is validated separately. Search may choose different answers and does not
review clue fairness. Fully checked layouts remain a comparison. Their failure does not
mean no playable crossword exists. See [the split results](fill-benchmark.md).

## Next gate

Blind-solve the Medium trial before constructing the other five pilot puzzles. Record
time, assistance, ambiguous clues and the usefulness of crossings. One complete fill and
one positive solve do not establish sustainable daily production.
