## Scope and interpretation

- all-triads: 100 eligible triads, 100 distinct grid answers.
- proper-names-only: 55 eligible triads, 55 distinct grid answers.

The proper-names-only control is a subset of this same sourced seed. It is not the
historical unrestricted word list. Both cohorts use identical layouts, seeds and limits.
Draft/rejected entries are excluded. Evidence-checked research entries remain
unpublished.

These original layouts sample open grids, clipped corners and blocked bridges/waists.
They are diagnostic fixtures, not an exhaustive or representative survey of all
crossword layouts. Symmetry is reported, not required. No unrestricted dictionary words
are added.

Unsatisfiable means this exact bank/layout is ruled out by a distinct-word length
shortage or exhausted constraint search. Budget-exhausted means unvisited branches
remain. Repeated seeds that stop before branching do not supply independent evidence.
These results cannot establish a daily size, puzzle quality, or a sustainable number of
dailies.
