## Scope and interpretation

Partially checked grids are the primary construction format. Fully checked layouts
remain a comparison. Both use the same bank, search algorithm and budgets. Each layout
declares its checking rule. The groups contain different shapes and are not a controlled
success-rate comparison. The partial layouts below came from authored puzzles. They are
positive controls and regression cases, not an unbiased estimate of generation success.
The bank-* layouts were fitted to this exact bank by a search, so their solves are
controls too, not a fill rate. Every fill also obeys the twin rule: an answer spelt out
in another's clue, hint or fact, cited from the same row, or the head or tail of another
(NOVA, FROSTNOVA) never shares a puzzle. A solved benchmark fill may choose different
answers from the authored puzzle. It proves structural fillability, not clue fairness.

The proper-names-only control is a subset of this same sourced seed. It is not the
historical unrestricted word list. Both cohorts use identical layouts, seeds and limits.
Draft/rejected entries are excluded. Evidence-checked research entries remain
unpublished.

The fully checked comparison samples open grids, clipped corners and blocked
bridges/waists. They are diagnostic fixtures, not an exhaustive or representative survey
of all crossword layouts. Symmetry is reported, not required. No unrestricted dictionary
words are added.

Unsatisfiable means this exact bank/layout is ruled out by a distinct-word length
shortage or exhausted constraint search. Budget-exhausted means unvisited branches
remain. Repeated seeds that stop before branching do not supply independent evidence.
These results cannot establish a daily size, puzzle quality, or a sustainable number of
dailies.
