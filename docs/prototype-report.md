# Easy construction trial

Generated by `pnpm prototype:build`. Research only, with no cold-solve publication
approval.

Layout: 12 columns × 13 rows. 12 entries, 47 white cells, 16 crossing cells.

Checking below is per answer. A crossing is counted in each of the two answers it helps.
Every entry has at least two crossings and 40% checking. This remains a provisional

## Review and feedback

The owner reports completing this puzzle blind, finding it interesting but easy. No
solve time or assistance counts were supplied. This is one player's feedback, not
publication approval.

The oversized heading, promotional copy and process commentary were removed after
feedback. Instructions remain available on demand.

The shape is irregular. The longest answer has a three-letter stretch without crossing
help, and some five-letter answers have two checked letters.

## Benchmark interpretation

Partially checked grids are the primary format. The fill benchmark now includes this
exact layout and independently searches the bank for a complete fill. The authored
placement is validated separately. Search may choose different answers and does not
review clue fairness. Fully checked layouts remain a comparison. Their failure does not
mean no playable crossword exists. See [the split results](fill-benchmark.md).

## Next gate

Blind-solve the Medium trial before constructing the other five pilot puzzles. Record
time, assistance, ambiguous clues and the usefulness of crossings. One complete fill and
one positive solve do not establish sustainable daily production.
